From 13 to 18 September 2026 **Dr. Wojciech Cygan** from the University of Wrocław (Uniwersytet Wrocławski) visited FER. Together with Asst. Prof. Stjepan Šebek, PhD, he worked on fluctuation laws of random walks with drift. Such results are part of the mathematical groundwork of VALOR.

The visit is part of building VALOR's collaboration with the University of Wrocław.
