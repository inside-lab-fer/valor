title: "VALOR project launched"
ref: start
summary: "A four-year project on interpretability and value alignment of large language models started at FER on 1 October 2025."
---
On 1 October 2025 the University of Zagreb Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing launched the institutional research project **VALOR – Value-aligned and interpretable optimization and reasoning**. The project runs for 48 months and is led by Assoc. Prof. Mario Brčić, PhD (Department of Applied Computing).

The team brings together researchers from three FER departments (ZPR, ZPM and ZEMRIS) and external collaborators, and works on interpretability and value alignment of large language models. The project is funded by the National Recovery and Resilience Plan 2021–2026 (NextGenerationEU).
